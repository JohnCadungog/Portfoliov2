# Portfolio home (all three career routes)

Scope: `/`, `/engineer`, `/design`, `/automation` — one surface, three lenses. Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience: recruiters and freelance clients, often on phone, deciding in under a minute.
Action: see real work, then email John or download the résumé.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The careers are swatches in one fan deck. Choosing a career fans its card forward and floods the page with that ink. Refuses the segmented-tab portfolio with a neutral hero and a row of equal cards.
OWN-WORLD: Printer's swatch book. Drenched career fields (ultramarine 2B3BE5, process magenta D4006E, chartreuse C6E400) on cool white stock; every container is a swatch chip: a color/image field over a white label strip with the ink's code. Bricolage Grotesque display, Geist text, Geist Mono only for ink codes and dates.
STORY: One person, three inks. Visitor sees the deck, flips to their lens, sees real work, reads experience, contacts.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Whole viewport drenched in the active ink. Huge display headline top-left (career statement), pitch and two actions under it. The fan deck of three tall swatch cards pivots on a rivet at bottom-right; active card upright carrying the lead project image and name label. Drag the deck or tap a card to switch.
FORM: Swatch fan deck, candidate 7 of the grounded list, seed e3615abc.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
